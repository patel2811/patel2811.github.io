Drivers of Product Success & Revenue

Using SQL to analyze e-commerce purchasing behavior, product performance, promotions, ratings, and regional payment preferences.

Graduate Database Marketing Project
MKTG 638 · 2026

The Business Question

What factors drive product success and revenue across an e-commerce customer base?

The project used SQL to investigate purchasing behavior, product performance, discounts, ratings, revenue efficiency, and regional payment preferences.

Project Snapshot

Dataset

Simulated Amazon-style e-commerce sales data

Orders

50,000 transactions

Business Questions

5 SQL-driven research questions

Analytical Approach

The analysis moved from transaction-level filtering and aggregation to statistical testing and regional ranking, then translated the results into marketing recommendations.

01 · Filter

Isolated relevant transactions and customer groups using SQL WHERE conditions.

02 · Aggregate

Used COUNT(), AVG(), MIN(), MAX(), SUM(), and STDDEV() to summarize purchasing and revenue behavior.

03 · Compare

Created rating categories, compared revenue across product groups, and examined relationships between discounts and quantity sold.

04 · Rank

Used a subquery and RANK() OVER(PARTITION BY ...) to compare payment-method preferences within each customer region.

05 · Translate

Connected database findings to promotional, segmentation, cross-selling, and regional marketing strategies.

Analysis 01

Do discounts drive larger purchase quantities?

Purchasing behavior was analyzed using descriptive statistics and the correlation between discount percentage and quantity sold.

Key Finding

The analysis found a correlation of approximately 0.0014 between discount percentage and quantity sold, indicating essentially no meaningful linear relationship in the analyzed dataset.

Analysis 02

Do higher product ratings correspond to higher revenue?

Numerical ratings were transformed into three categories: Excellent, Good, and Poor. Revenue and order volume were then compared across the groups.

Key Finding

Average revenue per order differed by less than 1% across the rating groups, suggesting that rating level alone was not a strong differentiator of average order revenue in this dataset.

Analysis 03

Which payment methods are preferred by region?

Order counts were grouped by customer region and payment method, then ranked within each region using a SQL window function.

Key Finding

The analysis showed different leading payment methods across regions, including wallet in North America, UPI in Asia, debit card in Europe, and cash on delivery in the Middle East.

Analysis 04

Which product categories generate strong revenue efficiency?

Average revenue per order was calculated by product category and filtered using a performance threshold.

Key Finding

All analyzed product categories exceeded the $300 average-revenue-per-order threshold, with relatively similar performance across categories.

Key Findings

Discounts did not meaningfully increase quantity

The near-zero correlation between discount percentage and quantity sold suggests that broader discounting was not strongly associated with larger purchase quantities.

Ratings alone were not a major revenue differentiator

Average revenue per order was highly similar across the Excellent, Good, and Poor rating categories.

Revenue performance was diversified

Average revenue per order was relatively balanced across product categories rather than concentrated in one dominant category.

Payment behavior varied by region

Regional ranking showed different payment-method preferences, supporting localized checkout and promotional considerations.

Marketing Implications

The analysis translated the database findings into several marketing actions.

Targeted Promotions

Favor demand-driven and targeted promotions over assuming that broader discounts will increase order quantities.

Performance-Based Segmentation

Combine ratings with stronger performance indicators rather than using rating as a standalone segmentation variable.

Cross-Selling & Bundling

Use the balanced product mix as an opportunity for upselling, bundling, and targeted cross-selling.

Regional Payment Strategy

Adapt checkout and promotional strategies according to payment preferences within each customer region.
