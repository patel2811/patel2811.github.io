PROJECT 03 · MARKETING ANALYTICS

Consumer Segmentation Perceptual Mapping Brand Positioning

Repositioning Trader Joe's

Using consumer research, segmentation, and perceptual mapping to understand how shoppers perceive Trader Joe's relative to key grocery competitors.

Method Consumer Survey
Sample N = 71
Retailers 6
Tools Qualtrics + Enginius

01 / THE QUESTION

How is Trader Joe's positioned in consumers' minds?

The project examined how consumers evaluated Trader Joe's relative to Whole Foods, Walmart, Target, Costco, and Sprouts across quality, value, health and organic options, variety and uniqueness, and convenience.

The analysis then combined perceptual mapping and segmentation to identify a potential positioning opportunity.

02 / FEATURED OUTPUT

Trader Joe's Competitive Positioning

The perceptual map provides a visual representation of how respondents positioned Trader Joe's relative to competing grocery retailers.

03 / APPROACH

From consumer research to positioning strategy

The project moved through four analytical stages: measuring perceptions, reducing those perceptions into dimensions, identifying consumer segments, and translating the results into a positioning recommendation.

01 · Measure perceptions

Survey respondents evaluated six grocery retailers across five key attributes.

02 · Reduce dimensions

Multidimensional scaling transformed the consumer evaluations into a perceptual space.

03 · Segment consumers

Clustering identified groups with distinct priorities and trade-offs.

04 · Identify the opportunity

The perceptual map and customer segments were combined to identify a potential strategic direction.

04 / ANALYSIS 01

Two dimensions capture 92.1% of the variance

The MDS analysis retained two dimensions that together explained approximately 92.1% of the variance in consumer evaluations.

What this means

The resulting dimensions provided a simplified framework for understanding the attributes underlying shoppers' perceptions of the competing retailers.

05 / ANALYSIS 02

Trader Joe's competitive position

The perceptual map showed how Trader Joe's was positioned relative to the other retailers included in the analysis.

Positioning insight

The project characterized Trader Joe's as occupying an affordable-specialty position associated with quality, variety, and uniqueness, with weaker convenience associations than several competitors.

06 / ANALYSIS 03

Three consumer segments

The segmentation analysis identified three distinct groups based on the attributes consumers prioritized when choosing a grocery retailer.

Wellness Prioritizers

Consumers focused on product quality, health and organic choices, and uniqueness.

Budget Maximizers

Consumers focused primarily on value and price-performance.

Convenience Seekers

Consumers who prioritize speed, accessibility, and ease of shopping.

07 / TARGET OPPORTUNITY

Focusing on convenience

The project selected Convenience Seekers as the target opportunity because convenience could potentially be improved through operational and service changes while preserving Trader Joe's existing brand strengths.

Existing Strengths

Quality, uniqueness, variety, and an affordable specialty proposition.

Identified Gap

Convenience was comparatively less associated with Trader Joe's than with several competitors.

Strategic Opportunity

Improve speed and ease of shopping without fundamentally changing the brand proposition.

08 / STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATION

Repositioning around convenience

The proposed strategy focuses on making the shopping experience faster and easier while protecting the attributes that differentiate Trader Joe's.

Streamline the store experience

Improve navigation, signage, checkout speed, and queue management.

Expand convenience services

Explore pickup, delivery, and other services that reduce friction in the shopping journey.

Simplify the assortment experience

Consider quick-grab sections, meal bundles, and intuitive product groupings.

Communicate convenience

Make speed, ease, and time savings more visible in customer communications.

09 / KEY FINDINGS

What the research showed

92.1% of variance

Two dimensions captured approximately 92.1% of the variance in consumer evaluations.

Three consumer segments

Wellness Prioritizers, Budget Maximizers, and Convenience Seekers emerged from the segmentation analysis.

Convenience represented an opportunity

The project identified convenience as an area where Trader Joe's could potentially improve without abandoning its existing strengths.

Preserve core differentiation

The recommended direction maintained the brand's quality, uniqueness, and specialty positioning while addressing convenience.
